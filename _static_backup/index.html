Artisanal Philosophy

The Alchemy of True Neapolitan Pizza

At D’Molto, we reject shortcuts. Every pizza is a masterclass in slow food — respecting the natural rhythms of fermentation, authentic Italian ingredients, and intense woodfire heat.

01

48-Hour Cold Fermentation

Our sourdough base matures slowly for two days, creating a cloud-like, airy cornicione (crust) that is intensely fragrant, easy on digestion, and bursting with complex flavor.

High Hydration Dough
02

San Marzano D.O.P. Tomatoes

Grown in volcanic soils at the foot of Mount Vesuvius, hand-crushed with sea salt and cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil. Naturally sweet, low acidity, vibrant red perfection.

Campania Volcanic Soil
03

Fior Di Latte & Fresh Burrata

Crafted fresh for decadent meltability without grease. From whole creamy burrata bursting over hot pies to delicate fior di latte that yields the iconic cheese pull.

Fresh Daily Artisan Cheese
04

450°C Firebrick Stone Oven

Baked in under 90 seconds in our blazing stone hearth. The intense thermal shock caramelizes the dough, creates trademark leopard spotting, and seals in freshness.

Wood-Fired Blazing Hearth
Master Pizzaiolo’s Touch

Hand-Stretched with Reverence

We never use rolling pins or mechanical presses. Every dough ball is gently stretched by hand from the center outwards, preserving the delicate trapped air pockets that give Neapolitan pizza its legendary light-as-air crust.

Zero artificial preservatives or commercial dough conditioners
Caputo Tipo 00 imported Italian flour

Our Journey in Ludhiana

"Soon, these walls will witness first dates, family dinners, and countless smiles."

Before the doors opened at 130 Model Town Road, we sat inside the quiet room and imagined what D’Molto would become.

We didn't just want to build another restaurant in Ludhiana. We wanted to build a sanctuary — a place where friends linger over piping-hot slices, where couples celebrate quiet milestones over tiramisu, where children watch mesmerized as pizzas slide into the blazing oven, and where the rich aroma of roasted espresso beans fills the evening air.

"Right now, it’s quiet. Soon, these walls will witness first dates, family dinners, birthday wishes, endless conversations, and countless smiles. And we can’t wait to welcome them all." — From our early Instagram journal (@dmolto_pizza)
